1. What was run

The victim is a frozen pretrained causal language model. Each CSV row is one private sequence. The trusted victim concatenates M=N×B rows, right-truncates/right-pads to S, computes shifted next-token cross entropy, masks padding, and performs one global active-target-mean backward pass.

FILM

Support-constrained Pythia adaptation without the planned candidate-gradient-norm reorder stage.

Excluded

LAMP is absent. No model, tokenizer or prior was trained or fine-tuned.

7. Interpretation by method

DAGER

Best evidence: exact visible-prefix recovery for a singleton. Failure mode: endpoint omission, repetitive extras and sharp degradation as aggregation/rank grows. At the Pythia-160m boundary, support F1 falls to 0.351.

FILM adaptation

Best evidence: support F1 1.000 across all cells and the strongest autonomous RL in most aggregate cells. Failure mode: poor ordering/repetition. Required name: support-constrained/no-reorder adaptation until gradient-based reorder is implemented.

V2 autonomous

Best evidence: support F1 1.000 and meaningful phrase fragments. Failure mode: output cardinality and fragment assembly produce low autonomous set scores; runtime scales steeply.

V2 assisted

Best evidence: compatibility with the preserved artifact, including 0.800 singleton assisted RL and 0.602 at the 160m boundary. It is oracle diagnostic evidence only.

8. Limits and remaining blockers

Not yet a publication-grade final benchmark. Numerical reproducibility is strong, but the campaign still lacks held-out final rows, repeated independent victim aggregates, confidence intervals and enforced three-process victim→attacker→evaluator isolation.
  • Development-only: first development rows, seed 0, one aggregate per cell.
  • Process boundary: current development runner keeps victim, attacks and evaluator in one host process and passes in-memory gradient dictionaries. Restricted views exist and were hashed, but attack subprocess reopening is not yet physically enforced.
  • Evaluator boundary: predictions are frozen and receipted, but a separate evaluator process reopening the receipt is not yet the campaign default.
  • FILM identity/provenance: missing planned candidate-gradient-norm reorder; do not claim a complete FILM reproduction. The implementation computes a support hash in memory, but the development pilot does not persist that support hash into the artifact/receipt.
  • DAGER cardinality: it can emit more than public M; the set evaluator correctly penalizes this, but the adaptation’s output contract needs freezing.
  • V2 oracle: assisted scores use private truth and must never be promoted to autonomous performance.
  • No endpoint completion: causal final target behavior remains separately visible; no truth-assisted endpoint module was added.
  • No training: no victim/prior/tokenizer training or fine-tuning occurred.
Allowed claim: “The current implementation deterministically reproduces these eight seed-0 development results from byte-verified gradients and frozen predictions.”
Not allowed: “These are final expected benchmark accuracies” or “all three published methods have been fully reproduced.”
