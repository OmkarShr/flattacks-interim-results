What the results mean

DAGER-Pythia-adapted

A fused-QKV right-subspace extension, not official Pythia support. It achieves nearly exact singleton visible-prefix recovery, including “PNMMVD”. It misses the endpoint and emits an extra repetitive candidate. Aggregate quality falls sharply as rank grows.

FILM-inspired beam

Token-support F1 is 1.000 in every cell, but order is weak. This is the support-constrained/no-reorder adaptation, not a complete reproduction of FILM’s planned gradient-norm reordering.

V2

Exact support and meaningful fragments persist across the matrix. Autonomous set scores are low; truth-assisted assembly reveals stronger fragment compatibility but uses private truth.

What this does not prove

These are reproducible development measurements, not final benchmark estimates.

No uncertainty yet

One aggregate per cell, seed 0, no repeats or confidence intervals, and no held-out final pool.

Isolation not final

The development runner shares one process across victim, attacks and evaluator. Hashed views exist, but final three-process isolation is not yet enforced.

Adaptations must be named

FILM lacks reordering and does not persist its in-memory support hash; DAGER can over-emit; V2 assisted is oracle-only. Do not claim full native reproductions.

Additional victim-provenance caveat: toy tests support global-mean and padding behavior, but a protocol-level real-Pythia native-PAD versus EOS-as-PAD equivalence check is not yet evidenced; the model-state digest binds repository/revision/dtype metadata rather than actual weight bytes.
